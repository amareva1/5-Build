Workshops

Hands-on learning in editing, cinematography, and storytelling.

Film Editing Workshop

Learn how to transform raw footage into a polished film through hands-on editing exercises. Each session covers timeline organization, cutting techniques, pacing, transitions, sound design, and basic color correction. Participants will practice assembling their own short sequences using sample footage and explore how editing shapes the final story.

Schedule & Cost:

Workshops run Saturdays from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, with a cost of $25 per session.

Cinematography Workshop

Explore the visual side of filmmaking by learning how camera techniques shape the mood and meaning of a scene. Sessions include shot composition, camera angles, lighting setups, camera movement, and exposure. Participants will practice setting up shots, experimenting with lighting, and using visual techniques to communicate ideas on screen.

Schedule & Cost:

Sundays, 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM, with a cost of $30 per session

Storytelling Workshop

Develop original ideas into compelling stories through exercises in screenwriting, character development, plot structure, dialogue, and visual storytelling. Participants will create story concepts, write short scenes, and share their work for constructive feedback. The sessions encourage creative thinking while helping filmmakers strengthen their narratives and connect with their audiences.

Schedule & Cost:

Wednesdays, 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM, with a cost of $20 per session

Supporting the Collective

Every workshop fee helps fund the Indie Film Collective and keep its programs running. Contributions go toward workshop materials, equipment maintenance, space rental, and future film screenings and community events. By attending a workshop, participants invest in their own creative growth while helping provide more opportunities for independent filmmakers and artists to learn, collaborate, and share their work.

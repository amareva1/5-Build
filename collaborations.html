Collaborative Schedule

All collaboration sessions are held in the main community room and are open to all free of cost. Donations are appreciated but not required.

Monday — Idea Development & Brainstorming

6:00 PM – 7:30 PM

Members gather to pitch film concepts, discuss creative ideas, and form teams for upcoming projects.

Tuesday — Scriptwriting & Story Development

5:30 PM – 7:30 PM

Writers and filmmakers collaborate on screenplays, develop characters, refine dialogue, and provide feedback on story drafts.

Thursday — Production & Filming

4:00 PM – 7:00 PM

Teams work together on active film projects, practice camera techniques, experiment with lighting, and capture scenes.

Supporting the Collective

Every workshop fee helps fund the Indie Film Collective and keep its programs running. Contributions go toward workshop materials, equipment maintenance, space rental, and future film screenings and community events. By attending a workshop, participants invest in their own creative growth while helping provide more opportunities for independent filmmakers and artists to learn, collaborate, and share their work.

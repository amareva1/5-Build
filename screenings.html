Film Screenings

Showcasing independent films from emerging creators. We release a new film by a new creator each month, below is a list of our featured films this quarter.

  • Where the Light Ends — Directed by Elena Marlowe. Featured the first friday in October
  • Static Between Us — Directed by Julian Cross. Featured the first friday in November
  • The Things We Leave Behind — Directed by Camille Reyes. Featured the first friday in December

Submissions

We hold open submissions quarterly in December, March, June, and September. The film must not exceed 90 minutes in length, and must be an original work. You can email us your submission at "indiefilmscollective@gmail.com"
